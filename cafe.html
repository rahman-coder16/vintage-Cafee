Our Story

A calm café lounge with a vintage soul.

Opened in September 2024, Vintage Coffee Cafe is the first premium café lounge by Vintage Coffee Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of BSE-listed Vintage Coffee and Beverages Limited. [13]

We created a space for unhurried conversations, focused work sessions, weekend gatherings and quiet solo coffee breaks. Expect earthy interiors, attentive service, books, board games and a menu made entirely vegetarian.
